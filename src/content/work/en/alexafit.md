AlexaFit is a calorie-tracking app built around one idea: counting calories is easy, staying consistent is hard. You describe what you feel like eating and the AI nutritionist plans the meal — calories and macros included — and logs it for you. A what's-in-my-fridge recipe finder, guided fasting plans and side-by-side food comparisons keep the daily routine simple, and an optional program adds a real coach who reviews your meals every day.

We designed and built it end to end: native apps for iPhone and Android with a Pro subscription and free trial, and a fast website in English, Spanish and Romanian that explains the features, walks new users through setup step by step, sells the coaching program and sends visitors straight to the App Store and Google Play.
