Arena Blanca helps homeowners along the coast organise and redesign their spaces — from a single wardrobe to a full home. The website had to feel the way a finished Arena Blanca room feels: light, calm and quietly considered.

We built a bilingual one-page experience that introduces the founder, shows the studio's work in a gallery filterable by project type, shares client testimonials from Sotogrande, Estepona and San Pedro, and turns interest into enquiries through a simple contact form.
