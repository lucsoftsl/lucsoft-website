Bianca bakes everything to order from her registered home bakery in Pueblo Nuevo de Guadiaro. She needed a site that feels as handmade as her bread — and that takes orders for her, so she can spend her time baking instead of answering messages.

The site brings together a full online shop with dietary filters and allergen information, a live countdown to the next bake-day order cutoff, a calendar of the local farmers' markets she attends, and a journal for recipes and news. It works in Spanish and English and keeps WhatsApp one tap away for regulars.
